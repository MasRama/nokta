# Nokta design: 0.4

Status: approved (Ren picked features 1 to 4 from the 0.4 suggestions and
asked for lyrics of Indonesian songs, built to Claude's recommendation)
Date: 2026-10-28
Scope: five parts, each its own branch and pull request, in this order:

1. Lyrics for songs played in a browser
2. Choosing between several players
3. Focus mode: quiet notifications, long breaks, a shortcut
4. Privacy dots for the camera, microphone and screen sharing
5. Notifications in the notch

## 1. Lyrics for songs played in a browser

### What we found

Every lookup on Ren's laptop ended "not found", while LRCLIB has synced
lyrics for every popular Indonesian song we tried (Hindia, Tulus, Nadin
Amizah, Sheila On 7, Mahalini, Bernadya, Juicy Luicy, NOAH, Feby Putri).
The player was Chrome. YouTube in a browser reports the video title and the
channel, for example title `TULUS - Hati-Hati di Jalan (Official Music
Video)` and artist `TULUS Official`, so the exact lookup and the search
both miss.

### Sources considered

- **Musixmatch**: synced lyrics need a paid commercial licence; the free
  tokens other tools use are against its terms. Not used.
- **NetEase and other unofficial endpoints**: no agreement to use them, and
  they can disappear without notice. Not used.
- **LRCLIB** (current): open, free, has the songs. It stays the only online
  source; the fix is asking it the right question.
- **Local `.lrc` files**: the user's own files, for anything LRCLIB lacks.

### Design

- `lyrics/clean.rs` (pure): turns a track into up to three search
  candidates `(artist, title)`:
  - drops bracketed parts that are not part of the song name: `(Official
    Music Video)`, `[Lirik]`, `(Lyrics)`, `(Audio)`, `(Visualizer)`, `MV`,
    `HD`, `4K`, `(feat. X)` and the like, and anything after ` | `;
  - drops channel noise from the artist: ` - Topic`, `VEVO`, `Official`,
    `Music`, `Channel`;
  - when the title has ` - ` (or an en or em dash), also tries the left side
    as the artist and the right side as the title.
- `lrclib.rs` searches each candidate until one matches. A search result now
  matches only if its normalized title equals the candidate's and its artist
  contains the candidate's (or the other way round), and its duration is
  within 2 s when the player reports a length. Before, the first result in
  the duration window was taken, which could be another song.
- Local files come first and are not cached: `<music file>.lrc` next to a
  `file://` track (MPRIS `xesam:url`), then `Artist - Title.lrc` in the
  lyrics folder (`$XDG_DATA_HOME/nokta/lyrics` on Linux,
  `~/Library/Application Support/nokta/lyrics` on macOS,
  `%APPDATA%\nokta\lyrics` on Windows), compared case- and space-insensitively.
- The cache key gets a version, so the old "not found" answers are asked
  again once.

## 2. Choosing between several players

- `io.nokta.Media1` gains `Players` (`a(ss)`: bus name and display name, in
  the order they appeared) and `SelectPlayer(s)`. Both are additions.
- Selecting a player counts as that player having just started playing, so
  the existing rules keep working: it stays active until another player
  starts playing or it closes.
- The expanded notch shows the player's name under the title when there is
  more than one, with a small arrow on each side; clicking them or
  scrolling over the cover goes to the next or previous player.

## 3. Focus mode

- `[timer]` gains `quiet_notifications = true`: while a focus phase runs,
  GNOME shows no notification banners
  (`org.gnome.desktop.notifications show-banners`). Nokta turns it off only
  if it was on, and puts it back when focus ends, the timer stops, or the
  extension is disabled.
- `long_break_minutes = 15` (1 to 60) and `sessions_before_long_break = 4`
  (0 means never): after that many finished focus sessions the break is the
  long one, and the count starts again. Stop also resets it. `Timer1` gains
  `Sessions` (`u`, focus sessions finished since the last long break) and
  `LongBreak` (`b`).
- `shortcut = "<Super><Alt>f"` starts focus or stops the timer. GNOME binds
  keys only through GSettings, so the extension ships a schema with one key
  and copies the config value into it; an empty value means no shortcut.
- The settings window gets the new rows in its "Focus timer" group.

## 4. Privacy dots

A small coloured dot on the notch's left side, like on a phone:

- green: the camera is in use (`Shell.CameraMonitor`, public API);
- orange: an app records the microphone, or the screen is shared or
  recorded (GNOME's own privacy indicators in Quick Settings; read by
  checking for each, so a missing one is skipped).

`[behavior] privacy_dots = true` turns them off. Shown in every view; the
notch does not grow.

## 5. Notifications in the notch

- With `[notifications] in_notch = true` (default), a notification that
  GNOME would show as a banner shows in the notch instead: the app icon, the
  title and one line of the body, for `duration_ms` (5000). Hovering keeps it
  open; a click activates it like a click on the banner; the notch then goes
  back to what it showed.
- Critical notifications and anything while the notch is hidden
  (fullscreen) go to GNOME's banner as before. Notifications still land in
  the message list; Do Not Disturb and per-app settings are respected
  because Nokta only takes requests GNOME would have shown.
- Like the OSD takeover, the hook wraps one Shell method, checks for it
  first, and is removed in `disable()`.
- `[notifications] show_body = true` hides the body for privacy when off.

## Testing

Each part: tests first for the pure logic (Rust and Node), the devshell for
the UI with screenshots, and a smoke check per part in the GNOME 46 to 51
CI matrix. Items that need real hardware (a webcam, Ren's browser) are
listed for Ren to try.

## Out of scope

Other lyric providers, word-by-word lyrics, notification actions in the
notch, history of focus sessions.
